🚀 User Guide & Features

Welcome to the ultimate space exploration experience. Here is everything you need to know to navigate, learn and play on this website.

🪐

3D Solar System Simulation

Explore our solar system in full 3D. Learn about planets, moons and their orbits in real-time.

  • Navigation: Click and drag to rotate the camera. Scroll or pinch to zoom in/out.
  • Information: Click on any planet or moon to read its scientific data.
  • Controls: Toggle Orbit lines, Asteroid belts and Space dust using the bottom menu.
Launch 3D Simulation
🌍

Interactive Earth Globe

Take a closer look at our home planet with a highly detailed, interactive 3D globe.

  • Search: Use the search box on the left to find any country instantly.
  • Rotation: The globe auto-rotates, but you can drag it to look around manually.
  • Borders: See the real-time country borders mapped onto the globe.
Explore Earth 3D
👾

Cosmic Shooter Game

Defend the Earth from 20 waves of alien invaders, including 4 massive bosses!

  • Controls (PC): Move your mouse to steer the ship. It fires automatically.
  • Controls (Mobile): Use the left and right on-screen buttons to move.
  • Power-ups: Collect 'W' for triple shots, 'S' for a shield and '+' for health repair.
  • Progression: Destroy ALL aliens in a level to proceed. Defeat bosses every 5 levels.
Play Space Shooter
🧠

Space Quiz Challenge

Test your knowledge of the cosmos with 50 mind-bending space questions.

  • Gameplay: Read the question and select the correct answer from the 4 options.
  • Streaks: Answer correctly in a row to build up a fiery streak!
  • Rewards: Score high to unlock ranks like "Cosmic Commander" or "Galactic Overlord".
Take the Quiz
⚖️

Planet Comparison Tool

Compare two planets side-by-side to see which one is bigger, hotter, or has more moons.

  • Select: Use the drop-down menus to pick any two planets.
  • Data: See real-time stats on Mass, Gravity, Temperature and Moons.
Compare Planets
📸

Gallery & Documentaries

Immerse yourself in the beauty of the universe through real images and videos.

  • Gallery: View and download high-definition space photos from NASA.
  • Videos: Watch curated space documentaries directly on the site.
